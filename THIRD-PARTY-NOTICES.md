# Third-Party Notices

ITAMbox redistributes the following third-party frontend components inside
the container image's `static/dist/vendor/` tree (see
`itambox/copy-vendor.mjs`). Each entry below names the package, the exact
version pinned in `itambox/package-lock.json`, its SPDX license identifier,
the copyright line(s) exactly as they appear in the package, and the
`LICENSES/` file(s) carrying the license terms. Where a package ships its own
LICENSE with a package-specific notice or a filled-in Apache appendix, that
package is called out explicitly.
